Who We Are

Built to Modernize the Traditional Jewelry Business

Aureate was founded to build software tailored exclusively for the operational nuances of retail jewellers.

Our Mission

For decades, independent jewelry store retailers have managed billions in high-value gold and gem inventories using paper ledgers or generic, complex ERPs. We believe that jewellers deserve simple, modern tools built for their specific requirements: dynamic metal rate pricing, Karat purity management, wastage adjustments, and secure tenant environments.
